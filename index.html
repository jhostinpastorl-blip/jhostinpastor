HOW I DESIGN AUTOMATION

Automation starts with a decision, not a tool.

Business problem→Integration boundary→Execution model→Reliability→Operations
01

Stable supported API?

Prefer direct integration when it is reliable, governed and sufficient.

02

UI interaction required?

Use RPA where the interface is genuinely the appropriate execution boundary.

03

Complex reusable logic?

Move it into Python/C# services or components instead of growing fragile workflows.

04

Real ambiguity?

Introduce AI with schemas, guardrails and human control—not because it is fashionable.

ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

Choose the simplest reliable boundary.

I do not treat RPA, code or AI as competing identities. They are execution options. The goal is to understand the business constraint, select the appropriate boundary, keep state recoverable, make failures observable and avoid adding complexity without a reason.
